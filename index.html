About

Why a teenager's review is worth reading.

I'm Tyler Simons, a 17-year-old developer and maker. I run local AI on a machine I assembled, I 3D-print enclosures for hardware projects, and I've been taking electronics apart since before I could buy my own screwdriver bits.

That background is the reason my reviews read differently. I don't summarize spec sheets — I print with the printer, charge with the power bank, and disassemble the laptop. I know what a Torx screw feels like when the bit doesn't quite seat, and I know what a slicer setting does to a overhang when you get it wrong. That's the kind of detail a PR contact remembers.

I'm not a publication with an editorial calendar. I'm one person who cares about getting the details right — which is exactly the kind of reviewer small and indie brands should want.
